# EV Market Performance & Insights

## Project Overview
An interactive Tableau dashboard designed to analyze the Electric Vehicle (EV) market, identify sales trends, compare manufacturers, and explore battery technologies and vehicle performance.

## Project Objectives
- Analyze EV sales across vehicle categories and market regions.
- Compare manufacturer performance and market presence.
- Explore battery technology, capacity, and driving range.
- Understand customer satisfaction across vehicle segments.
- Examine CO₂ savings and battery degradation patterns.

## Stakeholder Scenarios

### 1. EV Market Strategy Director
Understand sales trends, high-growth vehicle categories, customer satisfaction, and driving-range performance.

### 2. Automotive BI Manager
Compare manufacturers, market regions, manufacturing countries, and battery technologies.

### 3. Sustainable Mobility Research Analyst
Explore battery technology, driving range, CO₂ savings, and other sustainability-related indicators.

## Dashboard Visualizations
The dashboard includes visualizations covering:
- CO₂ savings by vehicle category
- Average driving range by vehicle category
- Battery capacity vs. driving range
- Manufacturing countries by sales
- Battery degradation by battery type
- Average customer rating by vehicle category
- Vehicle category vs. annual sales
- Sales by market region
- Top 10 manufacturers by sales
- Annual sales trend
- Vehicle distribution by category

## Dataset Notes
The dataset is synthetic and should not be interpreted as actual EV industry statistics. `model_year` represents the vehicle model year, not necessarily the year in which sales occurred.
